### 🔬 About Me
 
I am a Ph.D. student in Biological Sciences investigating how climatic variability and genomic features shape the transmission dynamics of zoonotic diseases. Working within a One Health framework, my research integrates high-throughput sequencing, viral gene dynamics, and ecological modeling of arthropod vectors and emerging pathogens. I leverage R and computational biology workflows for genomic epidemiology and reproducible data analysis.
